About

The company behind the clock.

D4D Sàrl is a Swiss company building Clockchain: a decentralized, cryptographically verifiable global time standard, anchored on-chain every second.

Building trust through verifiable time

Every digital system depends on one assumption: that time is accurate.

From financial transactions and AI decision-making to digital evidence and smart contract execution, timestamps determine the order of events, establish accountability, and enable trust. Yet today's digital infrastructure relies on fragmented and often unverifiable time sources that can drift, be manipulated, or become inconsistent across systems.

Clockchain was created to solve this challenge.

Clockchain is pioneering a new category of digital infrastructure: a decentralized, blockchain-based global time network that provides a cryptographically verifiable, independently auditable proof of time. By combining multiple trusted global time references with decentralized consensus, Clockchain creates a resilient, tamper-resistant time standard that organizations can trust for their most critical applications.

Our mission is simple: to establish a universally trusted standard for verifiable time.

Why time matters

Nearly every digital interaction depends on the integrity of time.

Every financial trade, AI decision, software log, blockchain transaction, sensor reading, and legal record relies on timestamps to determine when something occurred. If those timestamps are inaccurate, inconsistent, or vulnerable to manipulation, the integrity of the entire system can be compromised.

Traditional timestamping methods often depend on centralized servers or individual system clocks. These approaches can introduce inconsistencies, create single points of failure, and make independent verification difficult.

As artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, digital finance, and interconnected infrastructure continue to expand, trustworthy time is becoming as fundamental as secure identity and reliable networking.

Clockchain was built to provide that foundation.

What makes Clockchain different

Clockchain introduces a new approach to trusted time.

Rather than relying on a single authority, Clockchain continuously aggregates multiple independent, trusted time sources and establishes a unified network time through decentralized consensus. The resulting timestamp is permanently recorded on the Clockchain Network, creating an immutable and independently verifiable proof of when an event occurred.

This approach delivers:

  • Independent verification of time
  • Tamper-resistant timestamp records
  • Cryptographic proof of event ordering
  • High availability through decentralized architecture
  • Transparent, auditable historical records
  • A common time reference across organizations and jurisdictions

Clockchain is designed to complement, not replace, existing time synchronization technologies by adding a layer of verifiable trust.

Our vision

The internet standardized communication. Blockchains standardized decentralized trust. Clockchain seeks to standardize verifiable time.

We believe trustworthy time will become foundational infrastructure for the next generation of AI, finance, autonomous systems, and digital governance. As these technologies become more interconnected, organizations will require a shared, independently verifiable understanding of when events occurred, not simply a timestamp generated by an individual device or server, but one that can be validated long after the event itself.
